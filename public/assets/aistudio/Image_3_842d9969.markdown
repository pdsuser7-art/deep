## Brand & Style
The design system targets revenue leaders, sales compensation administrators, and commercial executives operating within high-growth Indian enterprises. The core visual philosophy pairs enterprise rigor with executive clarity: high-density financial metrics must remain instantly parseable, trustworthy, and actionable.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate Precision** with subtle **Refined Glass Elevation**. It emphasizes structured tabular grids, crisp structural boundaries, and disciplined informational hierarchy over decorative excess. The UI projects mathematical precision, operational authority, and transparent financial governance—evoking the reliability of a tier-1 banking terminal alongside the responsiveness of modern analytical software.

## Colors
The palette is built around executive contrast and rigorous semantic validation:

- **Foundation & Surfaces**: 
  - Primary Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) establishes a soft, anti-glare operational foundation.
  - Card & Table Canvas: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) provides clinical contrast for numeric data.
  - Sub-surfaces & Metric wells: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100).
- **Core Tonal Spine**:
  - Ink Primary (Headings, critical totals): `#0F172A` (Slate 900).
  - Ink Secondary (Table headers, structural labels): `#334155` (Slate 700).
  - Ink Tertiary (Footers, inactive metadata): `#64748B` (Slate 500).
  - Structural Borders: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for standard partitions; `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) for grid dividers.
- **Accents & Telemetry**:
  - Primary Brand & Action: `#2563EB` (Royal Blue) driving primary navigation, bulk recalculations, and active focal states.
  - Performance & Target Surplus: `#059669` (Emerald 600 base) with `#10B981` (Emerald 500 accent) reserved strictly for revenue goal completion, incentive payouts, quota overachievement, and positive delta adjustments.
  - Alert & Deficit: `#DC2626` (Red 600) for quota shortfalls, clawbacks, and validation discrepancies.
  - Carryover & Pending: `#D97706` (Amber 600) for pipeline holds, unapproved overrides, and rollover balances.

## Typography
Typography is split into two specialized roles:
1. **Primary Interface (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Provides razor-sharp, geometric legibility across UI components, executive summaries, target hierarchies, and form controls.
2. **Numeric & Formula Layer (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Mandated for currency figures (INR `₹`), commission percentages, multiplier bands, calculation logic expressions, and data grid cells. The monospaced width eliminates column jitter during live data feeds and allows frictionless vertical visual scanning across large matrices.

Indian numerical formatting conventions (e.g., `₹12,45,000.00` with Lakhs and Crores) must always align to tabular numerical figures with right-alignment in data matrices.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 12-column adaptive fluid grid engineered for dense, calculation-intensive dashboards:

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` safe outer margins. Maximizes horizontal space to accommodate multi-tiered commission tiers, rep lists, and monthly actual vs. target breakdowns side by side.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Complex tables shift to sticky left identifier columns (Rep ID / Tier) with horizontally scrollable metric cells.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Metric cards collapse to stacked KPI units with micro-charts.

Spatial rhythm leans compact (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 12px) within data surfaces to optimize vertical viewable real estate for enterprise analysts.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is established primarily through **structured micro-borders and soft ambient shadows**, avoiding heavy blur traps that degrade data density:

- **Base Level (Canvas)**: `#F8FAFC` flat surface.
- **Surface Level 1 (KPI Panels & Data Containers)**: Crisp solid background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Subdued elevation: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Modals, Formula Editors, Active Row Drawer)**: Refined frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) over `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) and directional elevation: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Context Menus, Formula Tooltips)**: `#0F172A` deep solid tone with zero backdrop blur, contrasting against light data grids for instantaneous focus: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15)`.

## Shapes
A disciplined **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** standard is enforced across the system:
- Controls, table rows, and compact metric chips utilize `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- Executive KPI cards, modal sheets, and data grid outer wrappers use `0.5rem` (8px, `rounded-lg`).
- Tab pills and numeric status markers utilize strict rectangular or subtle 4px corner radii—circular pill styles are strictly prohibited to maintain architectural stability and high tabular density.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#2563EB` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, 4px border radius, 36px standard height (`space-sm` vertical, `space-md` horizontal padding). Focus ring: 2px offset with `#93C5FD`.
- **Secondary / Action**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: `#F8FAFC`.
- **Export / Action Bar**: Monospaced utility triggers with 1px borders, incorporating export icons alongside plain Indian currency signifiers.

### Data Tables (High-Density Excel Style)
- **Header Cell**: 32px height, `#F1F5F9` background, uppercase `#334155` text at `label-sm`, bottom border 2px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- **Data Cell**: 36px height, compact 8px horizontal padding, 1px bottom border `#E2E8F0`. Tabular monospaced font for figures.
- **Zebra & Hover**: Alternating `#FFFFFF` and `#FBFDFE`; row hover highlights in `#EFF6FF` (Royal Tint) with a 2px left border accent in `#2563EB`.

### Metric Chips & Status Indicators
- **Achievement Surplus**: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`. Prepended with upward delta indicator (`+14.2%`).
- **Deficit / Clawback**: Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border 1px solid `#FECACA`.
- **Carryover / Hold**: Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border 1px solid `#FDE68A`.

### Formula & Rule Builder
- Visual rule canvas blocks enclosed in 1px `#CBD5E1` containers, using `#F8FAFC` headers. 
- Math operators (`+`, `-`, `*`, `MIN`, `MAX`) rendered in dark slate tags (`#0F172A`) with monospaced text to emphasize structural integrity.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height: 36px. Border: 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Focus: `#2563EB` border with 0 0 0 1px outline. 
- Integrated currency adornment: Hardcoded `₹` prefix locked in `#64748B` on the leading inline edge.
